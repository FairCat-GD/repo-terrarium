## Exception: bitmap fonts

The bitmap fonts are in the public domain. They are exact subsets of the `4x6` and `5x7` BDF
fonts from Markus Kuhn's `ucs-fonts`, whose headers state "Public domain font. Share and enjoy."
This covers:

- `art-assets/biome-temperate/ui/raw/batch-07/`: the BDF sources, with their `README` and
  `AUTHORS`;
- `art-assets/biome-temperate/ui/exports/batch-07/body-4x6.fnt` and `headings-5x7.fnt`;
- `art-assets/biome-temperate/ui/exports/batch-07/fonts-batch-07.png` and `fonts-batch-07.json`;
- every `tmp.font.*` glyph in `art-assets/biome-temperate/ui/exports/batch-07/individual/`.

Their source record is `art-assets/biome-temperate/ui/raw/batch-07/LICENSE-SOURCE.md`.
